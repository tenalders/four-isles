# The Silence of Songs — story outline (working)

**Tales From The Four Isles** · Philip Wahl

---

## World

| Isle | Notes |
|------|--------|
| **Isle of the Deer** | Bramble Town, royal castle, Hazel's home |
| **West-Moor-Lands** | Moors, Port Town, northern mines, Fionnula's cottage |
| **Greatwood Isle** | Ancient forest, griffin territory |
| **The Lost Isle** | Volcano / Mountain of Fire; dragon's lair; hidden by mist and ash |

**Deep Roads:** Pre-breaking tunnels connecting all isles underground. Grimrok's map: northern mountains → Crystal Caves → Underground River → warm middle branch → heart of Lost Isle volcano.

**Magic:** Songs are woven into the world. Bards sing *word spells*; mythical creatures hold elemental song-lines (sea, sky, earth). Petrification preserves creatures when songs are stolen. New songs (not only old ones) can restore balance.

## Thematic through-lines

- **Creation vs. preservation:** Dragon freezes song-keepers to "save" music; Hazel proves new songs heal the world.
- **Grief and connection:** Dragon's frenzy is loneliness, not evil; Holly left; song forgotten.
- **Each hero's gift:** Tracker, guardian, wise one, singer — repeated in every freeing riddle; climax uses Fionnula's gear + Hazel's creation.
- **Grandmother's legacy:** Holly adventured, knew Fionnula, loved the dragon's song; compass carries the key.

---

## Dragon reveal (canon for Ch. 14–15)

- Dragon and **Holly** were friends when she traveled the isles (including Lost Isle).
- She sang him a **particular song** (same melody as Fionnula's compass).
- She returned home to raise her family; decades passed; dragon **forgot** the song.
- Believing music was dying, he petrified mythical creatures (sources of elemental song) to preserve them.
- Only **Hazel** still knows the song (via compass); singing it snaps him out of frenzy.
- Resolution: release all stone creatures; songs return; dragon grieves but heals; Hazel honors Holly by making *new* music.

## Ch. 14 — The Dragon's Song (outline)

- Volcano heart: rows of petrified mythical creatures.
- Dragon appears: huge, grieving, defensive — **kid-friendly** conflict (no gore).
- **Alder:** shield dome protects party from breath / falling stone.
- **Clover:** fairy bow — warning shots, pin cloth or chain, create opening (never harm).
- **Huckleberry:** containment / slow spells from new book.
- **Hazel:** realizes compass = dragon's song; sings; dragon stills.
- **Reveal:** Holly's friendship; why he hoarded stone; apology and understanding.
- Dragon agrees to release prisoners when he remembers.
